Summary

TripSplit has no user account, no server of its own, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. I, the developer, receive no data from the app.

What the app processes and where

  • Trips, members, receipts, photos, itinerary: on your device and – if iCloud is enabled – in your private iCloud database at Apple (CloudKit). I have no access to it. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Shared trips: If you share a trip, the people you invite can see its contents through iCloud. Member email addresses are only used to add those people as participants when sharing through iCloud.
  • Receipt recognition: Text recognition and analysis with Apple Intelligence run entirely on your device. Receipt photos are not uploaded for this.
  • Camera and photos: only to scan or import receipts, to take or pick photos for itinerary items and – when you choose to – to save a receipt photo to your photo library, each only with your permission.
  • Location: only when you tap “I’m Here” or “Current Location” in the itinerary, and only with your permission – once, never in the background. To find the address and nearby places, the coordinates are sent to Apple MapKit. The place is stored only as part of the itinerary item (see above).
  • Exchange rates (optional): If automatic rate updates are on, the app requests daily rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.app, European Central Bank reference rates). Only currency codes and dates are sent; the service technically sees your IP address.
  • Address search: Place search in the itinerary uses Apple MapKit; the search text is sent to Apple. When navigating, the app opens the maps app you chose (Apple Maps, Google Maps, HERE WeGo) with the destination address.
  • Widget: Balances of the most recently opened trip are stored locally on the device in an area only the app and its widget can access.
  • Exports and backups: are created only when you ask for them. What you do with them is up to you.
  • Purchases (TripSplit Pro): Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. I receive neither your name nor any payment details. The app checks whether Pro is active using Apple-signed purchase records on the device.
  • Support requests: If you email me, I use your address and message only to answer your request and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

Retention and deletion

Data stays stored until you delete it in the app. Deleting the app removes local data; data in your iCloud can be deleted in your device's iCloud settings.

Your rights

As I receive no personal data from the app, I cannot disclose or delete any – it remains entirely under your control. For questions about data from a support request or about this policy, contact imakegreatapps@gmail.com.

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
